FAQ

Questions answered.

What kind of pet photos work best? +
Clear, well-lit photos where your pet's face is visible work best. Front-facing or three-quarter angles give the most detailed results. Avoid blurry or heavily shadowed photos. Don't worry if it's not perfect — we work with all kinds of photos.
How long does delivery take? +
Digital portraits are delivered to your email within 24 hours — usually much faster. Printed portraits ship in 5-7 days. Framed portraits ship in 7-10 days. Need it faster? Add rush delivery (+$20) for 12-hour digital turnaround.
What pets do you support? +
Any pet! Dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, fish, reptiles, horses, hamsters — if you love it, we'll paint it. We've done portraits of everything from tarantulas to tortoises.
Can I get multiple pets in one portrait? +
Yes! Multi-pet portraits start at $55 for digital. Just upload photos of each pet and let us know you'd like them together. Family portraits are one of our most popular orders.
What if I'm not happy with my portrait? +
We stand behind our work. If your portrait doesn't match the style you selected, we'll redo it once at no charge. Refunds are available for undelivered orders only — once a digital portrait has been delivered, we do not offer refunds as the product has been received. For printed and framed orders, contact us within 7 days of delivery if there is a quality issue with the physical print.
Is this a good gift? +
Furamous portraits are consistently rated as one of the most thoughtful, personal gifts people have ever given or received. Perfect for birthdays, holidays, anniversaries, Mother's Day, and as memorials for beloved pets who've passed.
